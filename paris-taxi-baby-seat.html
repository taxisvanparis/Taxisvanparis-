Frequently asked questions

Is a child seat mandatory in a Paris taxi?

In France, taxis are allowed to carry children without a seat, but we strongly recommend one. That is why we offer them for free.

How many child seats can you install?

Several. Tell us the number and age of your children when you book and we prepare the right seats.

Do I need to bring my own seat?

No. Our seats are certified. You can bring your own if you prefer.

Is there an extra charge?

No. Baby seats and boosters are free on every ride.
