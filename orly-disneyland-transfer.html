Frequently asked questions

How much is a taxi from Orly Airport to Disneyland Paris?

Our fixed price is €105 by sedan (1–4 passengers), €115 by van for up to 5 and €130 by van for 6 to 8. Price per vehicle, luggage and child seats included.

How long is the drive from Orly Airport to Disneyland?

About 50–60 minutes (around 55 km), depending on traffic.

Where do we meet the driver?

In the arrivals hall, after customs and baggage claim. Your driver holds a sign with your name.

What if our flight is delayed?

Give us your flight number: your driver follows the real landing time.

When should we leave Disneyland for our flight?

For an international flight, we suggest leaving Disneyland about 3 to 3½ hours before take-off.

Do you have baby seats?

Yes, free of charge: up to 2 baby seats and 2 boosters per vehicle. Just tell us the children’s ages when you book.
