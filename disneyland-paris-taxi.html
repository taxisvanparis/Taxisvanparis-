Frequently asked questions

How long is the drive from Paris to Disneyland?

About 40 to 50 minutes from central Paris, depending on traffic.

How much is a taxi from CDG airport to Disneyland?

It depends on the number of passengers. Send us your flight number and group size on WhatsApp and we reply with a fixed price.

Can we bring a stroller?

Yes. The van has a large boot for strollers and suitcases. Tell us in advance so we plan the space.

Can you pick us up after the fireworks?

Yes, we work 24/7. Book your return time in advance and your driver will wait for you.
