Frequently asked questions

How long does the taxi from CDG to central Paris take?

Usually 40 to 60 minutes depending on traffic and your address. Allow more on weekday mornings and Friday evenings.

Where will the driver meet me at CDG?

In the arrivals hall of your terminal (1, 2A–2G or 3), holding a sign with your name. Send us your flight number when you book.

What if my flight is delayed?

Our drivers monitor your flight’s arrival time and adjust the pick-up. The fixed price does not change.

Can I pay by card?

Yes, we accept cards and cash. The price is agreed before the ride.
