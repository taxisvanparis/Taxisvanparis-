Book in 3 steps: calculate your fare on our Booking page, choose your vehicle (sedan or van), then confirm online with your name and phone number: you're booked, no WhatsApp needed. Any question? Call or message +33 6 67 40 20 05. Available 24/7.
The Paris–CDG fare depends on your exact address and the time. By sedan, expect between €55 and €85 depending on your pick-up area. In a Mercedes V-Class van (up to 7 people), a small surcharge applies. Calculate your exact fare on our Booking page.
Yes, we provide transfers to all Paris airports: CDG (Roissy), Orly and Beauvais-Tillé. We track flights in real time so we are on time even if your flight is delayed.
Yes, we have certified baby seats (0–13 kg) and booster seats (15–36 kg), available in our sedans and vans. Just tell us what you need when you book on WhatsApp.
The Premium sedan seats up to 4 passengers with 2 to 3 suitcases. The Mercedes V-Class van seats up to 7 passengers with a large, flexible boot. Several sedans or vans can be booked at the same time on request, ideal for large groups and events.
Yes, Taxis Van Paris is available 24/7, including nights, weekends and public holidays. Our off-peak rates apply from 8 pm to 6 am and from 10 am to 3 pm.
We cover Paris and the whole Île-de-France region (75, 92, 93, 94, 95, 78, 77, 91), as well as long-distance trips across France and Europe. Contact us for a custom quote.
We accept card payments for any amount, with no minimum: Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Apple Pay and Google Pay, as well as cash. The fare is confirmed before the ride, so there are no surprises on arrival.
